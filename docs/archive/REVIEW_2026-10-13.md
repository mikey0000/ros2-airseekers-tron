## Majors

Control / MCU / docking / localization
- `cmd_vel_slew.py:216, 523` (b97de97): pivot assist re-enabled by default (0.14 m/s after 0.8 s, 0.3 m cap). It was retired on 10-07; the agreed fix was the RPP settings (3beca88, c079127), which are still in place, so the two run together. It sits downstream of collision_monitor, is gated by phase not lane (creeps during bumper rotate-clear, Spin recovery and teleop), and the slew ramps pass through the one-wheel zone for 5-6 ticks per pivot. Default off pending owner sign-off; if kept, nav lane only, shape after the slew.
- `mcu_node.py` neutral search: each pulse ends with one zero, not the 3-zero stop; `_moving` not set; a real `/cmd_vel` during a search gets a (0,0) appended.
- `mower_control/tilt_monitor.py` (fbb81bb): the "independent" CRITICAL stop is a 0.5 s zero burst; the emergency lane times out at 0.2 s, so the nav lane drives again after ~0.7 s unless the mission closes the gate. Keep publishing or latch a lock.
- `localization_status_logic.py:77-90`: monitor reports `ok` on receiver FIXED alone, even when fixes are not fused; `ekf_xy_sigma_m` never gated. `mission_fsm.py:3611-3625`: loc hold fails open on a hung monitor; a respawned monitor gives a permanent hold under float.
- `docking_node.py` `_on_rear_blocked`: latched Bool with no freshness; guard death fails open (False) or holds 60 s then DOCK_BLOCKED (True). The new rear corridor holds docking for a person between robot and dock even with the marker in view: defensible, contradicts the literal rule, needs an owner decision.

Mission / nav / map

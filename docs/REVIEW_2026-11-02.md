# System review 2026-11-02: what makes this platform rock solid

Master `3cfe973` plus the branches in flight that day. Five read-only reviews (architecture and
code quality, autonomy against the state of the art, vendor comparison, GUI UX audit with
Playwright against the simulator, correctness review of the week's 24 merges) synthesised into
one direction. Source reports were kept outside the repo; the numbers below come from them.
Earlier reviews: `REVIEW_2026-10-06.md`, `REVIEW_2026-10-13.md`,
`analysis/2026-10-21_independent_review.md`.

## 1. Verdict

The feature set already matches or beats the commercial RTK mowers: stripe, cross, spiral and
contour patterns, three edge laps with soft/hard modes, no-go zones with mid-mow re-plans,
per-area settings, skip-and-revisit, incident pins, a rolling bag ring, a simulator with a
nine-criterion gate, and a GUI that runs over Wi-Fi, Tailscale and Bluetooth. Against the stock
firmware we are clearly ahead on boundary safety, recovery budgets, docking cross-checks,
observability and link security.

It is not yet reliable unattended. Every field failure of the last week was a recovery failure,
not a feature gap: a stale heading refused a START, a side-slope boundary recovery slid into
STUCK_NEEDS_HELP, a dock request was refused for being off the route graph, a 38 m edge lap was
dropped behind three stone marks, the mower idled 92 minutes after MOWING_INCOMPLETE, and a night
dock needed five approaches. Three root causes sit under all of them:

1. **Single-antenna heading.** About 1 350 lines (`heading_logic.py`, `fsm/phases/hcal.py`) exist
   only to estimate a quantity a second antenna measures. The gyro offset has walked 4.8°/min.
2. **The simulator cannot fail the way the lawn does.** No roll, no load-dependent slip, RTK
   always fixed, no process kill. The slope and slip fixes landed unit-tested only.
3. **Too many interacting guards.** `Params` has 326 fields and `mission.yaml` 341 keys (133 on
   10-08); 46 are "false = old behaviour" flags whose old paths were never deleted; eight
   recovery-motion variants and about nine places decide what to do about an obstacle. Each new
   field incident added a stage instead of generalising one.

Nothing we built duplicates an upstream component we should swap today. Docking, routing and
coverage are field-tuned beyond what `opennav_docking`, `nav2_route` and `opennav_coverage` offer
on Jazzy. Two small adoptions are worth it (a Nav2 speed-filter mask for slope speed scaling,
`rosbag2 --snapshot-mode` for the black box) and one large one later (route tracking on
`nav2_route`).

## 3. Where the stock firmware is still ahead

From the decompile and vendor notes (`analysis/2026-10-21_stock_firmware_findings.md`,
`audit_2026-10-05/vendor_mission_layer.md`):

- Keeps mowing through RTK loss on stereo VIO plus visual relocalisation; we hold in place.
- Corrects heading about 5 Hz on 0.3 m chords; we take one fix per 0.6 m straight.
- Arc, fish-tail and slope turn modes; we pivot at the wheel deadband.
- Automatic edge mapping; voice prompts; battery-temperature and critical-battery gates; a
  "charger moved" notice; a camera-blocked monitor; OTA including MCU firmware; a post-task list
  of auto no-go zones with photos; Wi-Fi join over BLE.

Worth copying, cheap: RTK quality stamped on recorded vertices, stuck spots becoming keepouts,
re-mowing the ring around a removed obstacle, the battery-temperature and critical-battery gates,
charger-moved detection, a camera-blocked monitor, a day/night classifier for dock exposure.
Not worth chasing: VIO-through-RTK-loss (CPU is already at load 8-12; a graded RTK policy and the
LoRa base as primary corrections cover the real cases).

Inherited hardware we do not use: the magnetometer on the stereo module (a heading seed after a
reboot, if motor interference allows), the second IMU, hardware depth from the stereo chip, blade
current/rpm/temperature (a load-based slowdown and stall alert), the speaker, battery
temperature (display only), LTE except through the vendor service.

## 4. Direction: the next steps, in order

Each step has an acceptance criterion; a step is done when the criterion is met on the lawn, not
when the code merges. The overall target: **ten consecutive scheduled full mows with zero
operator interventions, zero STUCK_NEEDS_HELP, zero INCOMPLETE without a docked return, every
dock in at most two approaches.**

1. **Close the hazards H1 to H6.** Small changes, all unit-testable. Accept: a `docker kill` of
   the coverage server, the supervisor and the localization host mid-mow each produce a hold or a
   clean stop, never a silent continuation or an abandoned mow.
2. **Make the simulator able to fail.** Terrain roll and pitch with load-dependent wheel slip,
   `/tilt/status` published, RTK float and dropout injection, a mid-mow process kill, a night
   marker. Accept: the 11-02 side-slope trip reproduces at the measured 8-12° roll and the
   footprint recovery passes it on five seeds.
3. **Incident-to-regression loop and CI.** Every field incident becomes a bag-replay FSM test or a
   named sim scenario that is red before its fix. Seed with the 11-02 three plus the 10-28 ladder
   and dog-at-pivot. `ci_test.sh` runs pytest, the BLE and sim-gate scripts' tests, and one ideal
   gate. Accept: the five scenarios exist, red on the pre-fix commits, green on master.
4. **Dual-antenna heading.** Bench a UM982-class receiver against COG on a field drive before
   mounting; then fuse its heading as absolute yaw in both EKFs, keep `heading_logic` as a
   divergence watchdog, and delete the blind, pose-prior and stale-hold paths behind a flag.
   Accept: 20 STARTs (10 off-dock, 5 after a power cycle on the lawn, 5 after 30 min idle) with
   zero HEADING_NOT_ALIGNED and no calibration drive; GNSS-minus-COG heading p95 under 1°.
   Try the unused magnetometer first as a one-day experiment; it may be enough as a seed.
5. **Guard consolidation and parameter diet.** One recovery-motion planner
   (`body_boundary.plan_recovery` generalised with per-guard costs and the slip check) replaces
   retrace, inward, back, tilt back-out and pivot back-out. One slip/stall detector (motor
   current plus wheel-vs-RTK) for all phases, with escapes on the collision-monitored lane.
   Delete every revert flag older than two field runs and the superseded boundary stages
   (about 500 lines, 25 params). Remove `mower_proto`, `base_ble` and the off-by-default
   perception packages from the build (about 16k lines). Accept: `mission.yaml` under 200 keys,
   FSM lines down 15 % with the same tests passing and the gate equal or better.
6. **Unattended recovery after a reboot or crash.** Persist mission intent; on boot with intent,
   RTK fixed and heading OK, resume; otherwise dock. Never resume after EMERGENCY or a lift.
   Accept: five power cycles and five container kills mid-mow each resume or dock within five
   minutes with no GUI action.
7. **Graded RTK-loss policy.** Degraded: cap speed and finish the swath only if the drift and
   edge margins hold. Lost: hold. Over 120 s: retrace to the last fixed pose. Over 600 s: dock
   when fixed, else alert. A guard pause never charges the area's retry budget; the dock transit
   is exempt from the hold. Accept: scripted NTRIP cuts of 60 s and 5 min mid-swath finish the
   mow with zero operator actions and no boundary sample over 0.10 m.
8. **Docking from anywhere, day or night.** Seed the search heading from RTK and pick the marker
   solution nearest it; generalise dock-join to any lawn pose; matte or IR marker. Accept: 20
   consecutive docks including five at dusk and five from random poses 10 m away, zero failures,
   at most two approaches, median under 45 s.
9. **GUI UX batch.** See section 5: no-go sheet, hard boundary lines, the mobile layer list
   trapped under the activity sheet, a first-run Bluetooth setup guide, Wi-Fi network setup,
   plus the audit's top findings. Validated with Playwright against the sim before deploy.
10. **Operations hardening.** GUI image tagged by local commit with a rollback tag; the deploy
    script refuses a dirty tree unless told otherwise; `image` rebuild wired into the normal
    deploy; CPU cuts (map_server fed from odometry instead of `/tf`, MPPI batch 1000 to 600,
    cmd_vel_slew to C++ or into a critic); a coverage map from the blade footprint so "% mowed"
    is honest; rain sensor verified on hardware with a dock-until-dry mode.

Left out on purpose: the 2 cm weave (cosmetic, controller-side per the 11-01 A/B), swapping
docking, routing or coverage to upstream packages, and VIO.

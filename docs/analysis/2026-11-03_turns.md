# Row-end turns and pivot time (2026-11-03)

Question: the sim gate reports 27-30 % of moving time pivoting (criterion g). Can row-end
arcs, fish-tails or bulb turns cut that to 15 % or less?

Short answer: not through row-end turns. Row-end pivots are about 7 % of moving time on
real_run5. Half of the pivot time is at transit route corners and sub-path starts. With this
robot's 0.60 m nose and 0.45 m row-end inset, forward arcs do not fit at a row end, and an
in-place pivot is the fastest turn that does fit.

## 1. What exists upstream

- Fields2Cover: `f2c::pp` has `DubinsCurves`, `DubinsCurvesCC`, `ReedsSheppCurves` and
  `ReedsSheppCurvesHC`, all derived from `TurningBase`. `PathPlanning` chains swaths with one
  of these planners. There is no fish-tail or bulb class
  (https://fields2cover.github.io/api/f2c_library.html,
  https://github.com/Fields2Cover/Fields2Cover).
  **We already use it.** `planSwathTurn` / `planTurnIn` in `src/mower_coverage` call
  `DubinsCurves` (forward loop) and then `ReedsSheppCurves` (with reverse), and fall back to a
  pivot. Area setting `turn_type` = auto | loop | reverse | pivot, `min_turn_radius_m` 0.5,
  raised to the 0.40 m the shaper can drive. `route_order` racetrack (default) spaces rows
  (k-1)·w ≥ 2r apart, so a forward U-turn would fit between them.
- opennav_coverage wraps F2C v2: `default_path_type` DUBIN | REEDS_SHEPP,
  `default_path_continuity_type` CONTINUOUS | DISCONTINUOUS, `default_turn_point_distance`,
  `min_turning_radius` (https://github.com/open-navigation/opennav_coverage). It adds nothing
  beyond F2C's turn planners.
- OpenMower plans with slic3r_coverage_planner and follows the path with teb_local_planner
  (https://github.com/ClemensElflein/open_mower_ros). No dedicated U-turn modes were found.
- Stock Tron (docs/analysis/2026-10-21_stock_firmware_findings.md, "Edge-ring turns"):
  TurningMode Default / In_Place / Circular_Arc / Inner_Fish_Tail / Outer_Fish_Tail / Slope /
  Self_Adaptive. `NavCoverUturn` has `uturnInplace`, `fishExtern`, `fishInner` and
  `uturnArc`. The outer fish-tail goes: arc r 0.3 forward to perpendicular, 0.6 m straight
  reverse, arc into the next row. Stock arcs are tighter than our 0.40 m drivable minimum,
  stock lets the body overhang the line by up to half the width, and it forces in-place
  turns at path starts and ends.
- Ours: `path_conditioning.join_turn_chains` replaces swath-end Reeds-Shepp chains by
  swath → connector → swath (the 0.3-0.9 m reverse legs failed the tight goal checkers on
  2026-10-15). Corners are then filleted where an arc of r ≥ 0.5 fits and pivoted where it
  does not. The `FollowCoveragePathReverseMPPI` controller drives the reverse legs that are
  kept.

Adopt: nothing new. F2C's planners are already in place, and they are the useful part of
opennav_coverage. A stock-style fish-tail needs r 0.3 arcs (below what the shaper drives)
and accepts body overhang that the soft limit forbids.

## 2. Measured breakdown (real_run5, seed 2, -d, x3; review-2026-11-02/hazards/gate)

Episodes are ground-truth samples with |v| < 0.02 m/s and |w| ≥ 0.1 rad/s (gate criterion
g). Each episode is matched to the mission log's pivot reason by position, within 0.35 m.
Script: `classify.py`, inlined in the appendix.

| cause | n | s | % of moving (1022 s) |
|---|---|---|---|
| route / transit / dock corners | 23 | 116.0 | 11.4 |
| sub-path start after a transit (leg start) | 7 | 41.3 | 4.0 |
| row-end pivot vertex (≥ 100°) | 8 | 74.8 | 7.3 |
| edge-lap corner pivot vertex (< 100°) | 12 | 39.6 | 3.9 |

Pivots run at 14.5°/s (0.25 rad/s) in every class. Area 0 has 3 laps and 16 swaths, which
the planner turns into 17 sub-paths and 16 transits (11.4 m). Racetrack rows are 1.08-1.26 m
apart and their loops do not fit the 0.45 m turn inset. The snake fallback is no better, so
most row changes become blade-off transits, and each transit costs about 3 pivots (two
route corners and one leg start).

## 3. Row-end turn geometry (spacing d, R = 0.5, body front 0.60, rear 0.10, half width 0.235)

Row ends sit 0.45 m inside the line (soft layout). Soft planning limit: body ≤ 0.26 out.

| turn | path length | reach past row end (base / nose) | body out | time* |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| pivot 180° + 0.18 shift (d 0.18) | 0.18 m | 0 / 0.645 sweep | 0.195 | 13.4 s |
| bulb / Ω (d 0.18 or 0.36, needs d < 2R) | 3.22 m (d 0.36) | 1.23 / ~1.7 | > 1 | ~16 s |
| outer fish-tail (fwd 90°, rev 0.82, fwd 90°; d 0.18) | 2.39 m, 2 cusps | 0.5 / 0.95 | 0.50 | ~15 s |
| forward U-turn, racetrack d 1.08-1.26 | 1.7-2.0 m | 0.54-0.63 / ~0.95 | 0.50 | 6-9 s |
| current racetrack transit (pivot, drive, pivot, leg start) | 1.1-1.3 m | 0 | ≤ 0.2 | ~16-20 s |

\* Times assume arcs at 0.2 m/s, lines at 0.3 m/s, 1.5 s per cusp and pivots at 14.5°/s.

Bulb geometry: a right arc of 47°, a left arc of 274° whose centre is 0.73 m past the row
end, then a right arc of 47°. Every forward turn swings the nose about
sqrt((R + hw)² + front²) = 0.95 m from the turn centre. With the row end 0.45 m inside the
line, the nose reaches ~0.5 m outside it. Only the racetrack forward U-turn is faster than
the turn it replaces, and it needs about 1.0 m of room past the row end (rows 1.0 m inside
the line). That leaves a 0.55 m band for extra edge laps (+3 laps), which costs about as
much time as the turns save.

## 4. Prototype: `turn_mode` (commit e68df21)

`RouteOptions::turn_mode` = pivot | arc | fishtail | auto. It is a `mower_coverage_node`
parameter (launch arg `turn_mode`, default pivot) and is read per request.

- pivot: the turn region stays at turn_inset, exactly as before.
- arc / fishtail / auto (edge layouts only): base_link may go anywhere at least
  0.235 + 0.05 m inside the line. At every turn pose, each body corner may be at most 0.26 m
  outside the line. arc runs Dubins only, fishtail runs Reeds-Shepp, auto runs Dubins and
  then Reeds-Shepp. A turn that fails these checks becomes a pivot.
- gtest `CoverageTurnMode.*`: parsing; a loop is rejected 0.45 m from the line (nose) and
  accepted at 1.3 m; every planned turn pose of a soft-layout plan stays at least 0.285 m
  inside.
- The per-area setting needs a map_server / mission field that forwards it. That code
  belongs to other owners, so for now the setting is a node parameter.

Gate results: see section 5.

In auto mode the planner found one extra Reeds-Shepp fish-tail. `join_turn_chains` replaced
it with connector pivots, as it does every swath-end reverse chain ("0 kept as planned").
The result is two more row-end pivots and a different transit layout. Its 4 % shorter run
is ordering noise, and d and e regress. No forward loop fit anywhere, as section 3 predicts.

## 6. Recommendation

Keep `turn_mode` pivot (the default). Do not adopt arc, bulb or fish-tail row-end turns for
this body. Turns are cheaper only for racetrack rows, and only with about 1 m of turn room
past the row ends. The pivot share is set by the transit structure on small areas, where
each row change costs about 3 pivots. Next levers, owned outside mower_coverage:

1. Small areas: plan boustrophedon / snake with connector pivots inside one sub-path rather
   than racetrack transits. This removes the route-corner and leg-start pivots, about 15 % of
   moving time on real_run5.
2. Transit approach: end transits tangent to the next sub-path start (a Dubins final leg),
   which removes the leg-start pivots (4 %).
3. The pivot rate is capped at 0.25 rad/s (MCU 0.3). Each +0.05 rad/s cuts all pivot time by
   about 17 %.

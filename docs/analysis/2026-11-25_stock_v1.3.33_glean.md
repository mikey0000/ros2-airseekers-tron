# Stock v1.3.33: compatibility and what to take from it (2026-11-25, branch ws/glean1125)

The stock software v1.3.33-tron-rc.1 was installed on the mower on 2026-11-25. It flashed the
RTK rover board (0.9.50 -> 0.9.57) and the 4G modem (Quectel EC200A, beta
`EC200AEUV1HAR02A07M16_BETA0811`). The chassis (0.6.34), cutter (0.6.36) and BMS (1.18.0) are
unchanged. The base station still reports 0.0.0 / OTA_PENDING and was not updated.

Method: read-only. `strings`, `cmp`, `diff`, a disassembler on the vendor libraries, and our own
Python run with `python3 -I`. Nothing from the vendor trees was executed. The full vendor diff
is `v1.3.33/DIFF_v1.3.27.md` in the parent folder.

## 1. Compatibility verdict

### RTK rover board 0.9.57: compatible, no change on our side

- Sizes: 0.9.50 is 37560 B, 0.9.56 is 42432 B, 0.9.57 is 43148 B. The version strings match.
- The host command table of 0.9.50 (`$GLORA LORA_OTA $GHARD LORAOFF RTKOFF UNLOGRTK LOGRTK
  $GNRTK $GNCON RTKCLEAR $GNVER $GNSNR LORARESET $BRESET RTKRESET $MRESET LORARESETCOUNT $GLIST
  $GNSNW`) is unchanged in 0.9.57. Two entries were added: `$GOFIR` and `$GOFIW`.
- Everything we send is still there with the same bytes: `$GNCON,AAAA,CC`, `$GLIST,0|1|2|F`,
  `$GNVER,*64`, `$GHARD,F*32`, `$GNRTK,ON*69`, `$GNRTK,OFF*27`, `$BRESET*17` (`lora.py`,
  `rtk_pair.py`) and `RTKRESET` (`um960_node.py`). The reply formats we parse are unchanged:
  `GNVER,%s`, `GHARDM`, `$GNRTK`, `$GNCON`, `$GLIST`, `GNSTA` FINE/FAIL.
- The serial port is unchanged: 115200 baud (the vendor `mower_rtk.yaml` is byte-identical).
- The LoRa band table (863.2/927.4/902.5), the `NETID`/`LOCID` handling, and the GNSS module
  init lines (`SETRTKTIMEOUT`, `SETRTKRELIABILITY`, the RTCM3 lists, `PQTMCFGUART,W,921600`)
  are unchanged.
- New in 0.9.56 and 0.9.57:
  - `$GOFIW`/`$GOFIR`, a 5-field stored record that the vendor host never writes;
  - the `GN100`, `GNQUAB` and extra `GNREF` formats;
  - L76K module support (CASIC `PCAS` commands).
  Our parser drops unknown sentences. One cosmetic risk: if the board forwards its own `GNREF`
  form (`GNREF,<time>,<lat>,...`), `parse_gnref` would show a time in the `station=` status
  field. That field is display-only.
- NRTK throttle (`gps_msgs/Info.nrtk_mode`, `rtk.proto NRTKWakeupReq`, `/mower_gps_node/
  ntrip_wakeup`) is a host-side NTRIP policy. In the vendor `ntrip_client.cc`,
  `set_throttle_mode()` limits NTRIP to Wi-Fi (no 4G) and mutes the `NRTKSignalWeak` notice. The
  rover image has no throttle, wake or sleep strings. The board cannot stay "throttled" under
  our stack, and we do not need to send anything new.
- Rover 0.9.57 with an older base: no version check or mismatch string exists in either image,
  and the RTCM sets are unchanged. One inference: `GNQUAB` (base quality) moved from the
  station to the rover format strings, so the vendor app's base-quality readout may be blank
  with an old base. The owner uses NTRIP (`$GNRTK,ON`), which does not involve the base.
- Station 0.9.51 -> 0.9.57: adds `GFIO` and `RTK_STATION ERROR%s`. `$GNCON`, `$GLIST`,
  `$BRESET`, `$GNLOC`, `$GNSNR` and RTCM 1005/1006/1074/1084/1094/1124 are unchanged.
- Not verifiable from strings: whether the stored pairing (`$GNCON` address and channel,
  `$GLIST` band) survived the flash. Run `scripts/rtk_pair.py query` once after switching back.

### 4G modem (EC200A beta firmware): no effect on our stack

- Our stack never talks to the modem. There is no AT, ModemManager, APN, QMI or ECM code.
  `serial_lte` and `usb0` appear only in the udev rules, the compose device list and the docs.
- The new vendor scripts keep what anything could depend on: USB PID 6005, `usb0`,
  `/dev/ttyUSB2`, `AT+QCFG="usbnet",3` (ECM) and the same ICCID-to-APN map.
- New in the scripts:
  - the firmware flash (QDloader, which compares only the `AT+QGMR` version string);
  - a band check (`AT+QCFG="band"`, with `AT+QPRTPARA=3` when it differs);
  - `AT+QCFG="nat",1` and `AT+QNETDEVCTL=3,1,1`.
- A repair resets the modem (GPIO59, `CFUN=1,1`). `usb0` and `ttyUSB*` then re-enumerate for
  about a minute. Our containers do not open those devices.

### Network: the vendor base_net_node under our stack (documented, no code change)

`scripts/switch_stack.sh ros2` keeps `rosmaster` and `mower-network-auto` running, so the
vendor `base_net_node` keeps running. Its source is not shipped. Its strings show what it does:
- it adds, modifies and deletes NetworkManager Wi-Fi profiles (`deleteOtherWifiConnections`,
  `parkWifiProfile`);
- it locks the BSSID and sets a MAC denylist on its profile, turns firmware roaming off
  (`wl roam_off`) and uses `wpa_cli` bssid_ignore;
- it sets policy routing per interface and `rp_filter=2`, and runs `resolvectl` and restarts of
  systemd-resolved;
- on ping failures it runs `repair_wifi.sh` (restarts NetworkManager) and `repair_lte.py`.

It only serves `/base_net/*` services and has no subscriptions, so missing vendor nodes do not
put it into repair loops.

What it means for each piece of ours:

| Ours | Conflict? |
|---|---|
| `wifi_monitor` (reads `iw`, `/proc/net/wireless`) | None. Its roam counts now reflect base_net's BSSID lock and `roam_off`, so the 2026-11-01 roaming baseline predates this. |
| `mowgli-remote` Tailscale (userspace, `TS_ACCEPT_DNS=false`) | None. No tun device, so `rp_filter` and resolved restarts do not matter. LTE failover routing is now base_net's job. |
| GUI Settings -> Network Wi-Fi (NetworkManager over D-Bus) | Possible. base_net's "other connections" cleanup may delete a profile the GUI added (not verified). |
| Our own BSSID or band locks (the `wifi.md` plan) | Yes. base_net rewrites `802-11-wireless.bssid`. Do not set them by hand. |
| Docker host networking, DDS | Low. Source-address choice can change after a failover. |

Done: `docs/handover.md` (a new "What the vendor base_net_node does" block, with the checks to
run after a switch), a risk note in `docs/wifi.md`, and the `switch_stack.sh` header comment.
`mower-lte` is disabled by the v1.3.33 install, and LTE repair is now base_net's job.

## 3. Implemented

1. **Low voltage at a healthy SOC** (`fsm/guards/parity.py` `_battery_low_voltage_tick`).
   Mid-mission, `/battery` voltage <= `battery_low_voltage_v` 20.0 while the SOC is >=
   `battery_low_voltage_min_percent` 30 for `battery_low_voltage_hold_s` 5 s triggers:
   - a `battery_voltage` notice;
   - `LOW_BATTERY_DOCKING`;
   - CHARGING until min(100 %, `battery_max_charge_percent`), not the usual 95 %, then
     resume.

   0 V or a missing voltage counts as unknown. Setting the threshold to 0 turns the guard off.
   The 6S pack reads about 21.6 V at 52 %, and a brief blade spin-up dip is shorter than the
   hold. Tests in `test/test_stock_parity.py`:
   - dock, then resume only at 100 % (not at 97 %);
   - the resume target respects an 80 % charge limit;
   - no dock on brief sags, at a low SOC (the SOC guard's job), or with an unknown voltage.
2. **seg_ros default model `pp37.rknn`** (`seg.yaml`, the node default, and
   `install_models.sh` MODELS). The deploy step is in `src/seg_ros/README.md`. Copy
   `/workspace/src/mower_perception/seg_ros/model/pp37.rknn` to `/userdata/ros2/models/` on the
   mower, or push it with `MODELS_SRC=<v1.3.33>/src/mower_perception/seg_ros/model
   ./scripts/install_models.sh`. To go back: `model_path:=.../pplite-seg_20260630-1-6cls.rknn`.
   Seg stays off by default (`seg:=false`). Caveat: pp37's logit range is [-11.6, 12.1], against
   [-13.7, 16.4] for the old model. The top-1 softmax confidence therefore runs lower, and
   `nongrass_projector` `min_conf_nongrass` 0.8 may need re-tuning from a live overlay.
3. **Docs:** the base_net coexistence (section 1), the rover 0.9.57 note in `rtk_pairing.md`,
   and the `switch_stack.sh` comment.

## 4. Tests and sim

- `scripts/ci_test.sh`: PASS (colcon 3997 tests, 0 failures, 85 skipped; scripts 96 and replay 6 passed). The 3 new tests are in the colcon count.
- Sim: none run. No driving parameter changed. The low-voltage guard cannot trigger in the sim,
  whose battery model publishes `21.0 + 4.2 * soc` V (always > 20 V). It therefore returns
  early on every tick, the same as master. The unit tests cover its behaviour.

## 5. Left, with reasons

- **Bumper back-up safety check against the boundary and keepouts.** Our back-up reverses
  0.30 m without checking where the tail goes. The stock version sweeps the footprint over the
  static map and skips the escape when no backward shift is safe. Porting it means giving
  `bumper_controller` the keepout/global costmap and a footprint sweep, and deciding what
  happens after a skipped escape (hold for the mission's bumper-skip logic). It also needs a sim
  world with a bumper hit next to a hard edge, which does not exist yet.
- **Speeds** (transit 0.8, edge 0.45): an owner decision, plus a re-tune of the collision
  monitor and stop bands. Not a glean item.
- **To check on the mower:**
  - `rtk_pair.py query` (pairing and version after the 0.9.57 flash);
  - the base_net checks in `handover.md` (`ip rule`, `ip route show table all`,
    `resolvectl status`, `/userdata/log/net_manager.log`, Tailscale over LTE only);
  - whether a GUI-added Wi-Fi profile survives base_net;
  - the first pp37 overlay and the confidence gate.
- **Cosmetic:** the `parse_gnref` station field if the 0.9.57 board forwards its own `GNREF`.
  Stale "0.9.50" text in `docs/mowglinext_*.md` and in the `test_node_smoke.py` fixture (display
  only).
